# Calibrated confidence

Attach a confidence to every judgement, and keep the estimate separate from its
strength.

**Two different things.** "The lease was awarded in March" and "I am confident
the lease was awarded in March" are separate claims. The first is your estimate;
the second is how much weight it can bear. Reporting one without the other
leaves the reader to guess, and readers guess high.

**Say what drives it.** Confidence comes from the quality and independence of
what supports a judgement, not from how many documents mention it. Ten
documents repeating one source is one source. A single contemporaneous record
can outweigh them.

**Separate the reducible from the irreducible.** Some uncertainty could be
resolved by more searching — say so, and name the search. Some cannot be
resolved from this corpus at any effort — say that too. They call for different
things from the person reading you: one is a task, the other is a limit.
