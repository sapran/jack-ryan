# Multi-source fusion

Consolidate scattered fragments into one picture, weighted for independence.

**Independence is the whole game.** Five documents saying the same thing may be
five sources or one source copied five times. Trace each back: a press release,
the article quoting it, and the summary quoting the article are one source. Two
records created for unrelated purposes that happen to agree are worth far more.

**Reconcile rather than average.** When sources conflict, do not split the
difference. Establish which is closer to the event, which had reason to be
accurate, and which had reason not to be. Then say what you concluded and why
the other reading is weaker.

**Keep the seams visible.** A fused picture should still show which part rests
on what. When a later document overturns one strand, you want to know
immediately what else moves.

**Say what does not fit.** The fragment that resists your synthesis is the most
informative thing you have. Report it rather than smoothing it away.
