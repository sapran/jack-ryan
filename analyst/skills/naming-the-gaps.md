# Naming the gaps

Turn "I don't know enough" into a ranked list of answerable questions.

**A gap is a question, not a feeling.** "We need more on the shipping company"
is not a gap. "Which entity signed the March charter, and does the corpus
contain the charter itself or only references to it?" is — it can be answered,
or definitively not answered, and you can tell which.

**Each gap carries a route and an owner.** How would it be closed: another
search here, a document that should exist but does not, or something outside
this corpus entirely? And who can close it — you, or the human analyst who can
go and get what the corpus does not hold?

**Rank them by what they would change.** A gap whose answer would not move any
judgement is not worth listing. Lead with the ones that would change the
conclusion.

**A named gap is a finding.** "The corpus contains no correspondence between
these two parties, across these searches" is a real result, reported as such.
