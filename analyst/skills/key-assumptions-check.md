# Key assumptions check

Surface what your reading rests on but has not established.

**The method.** Write down what you are treating as true without having cited
it. Every investigation accumulates these: that a name refers to one person,
that a document is what it claims to be, that the corpus contains what would be
there if the thing happened, that a date is when something occurred rather than
when it was recorded.

**Mark the load-bearing ones.** For each, ask what happens to your conclusion if
it is false. Most assumptions do not matter. The ones that would collapse the
finding are the ones to test, and they are often the ones nobody thought to
state.

**Test what you can, declare what you cannot.** Some assumptions can be checked
against the corpus with one search. Others cannot be settled from these
documents at all — those belong in the brief as stated assumptions, not buried
as premises.
