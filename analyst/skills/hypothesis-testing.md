# Hypothesis testing

Hold every plausible explanation at once and score the evidence against all of
them, rather than building a case for the first one that fit.

**The method.** List the explanations that could account for what you have
found — including the dull ones, and including "this is a coincidence". For each
piece of evidence, ask not "does this fit my explanation" but "which
explanations does this *rule out*". Evidence consistent with everything tells
you nothing, however striking it looks.

**Hunt what would kill the leader.** Once one explanation is ahead, search
specifically for what would refute it. This is the search you least want to run
and the one that most improves the answer. In a document corpus it usually
means searching the other side's vocabulary: the objection, the denial, the
competing bid.

**Report it honestly.** Say which explanation the evidence favours, which
remain live, and what would distinguish them. An analyst who reports only the
leading explanation has thrown away most of what they learned.
